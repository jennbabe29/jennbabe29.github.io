New to Polymarket? Start here

Polymarket is a site where people bet on whether things will happen: elections, sports, the economy, and so on.

  • Every question has two sides, like Yes and No. Each side has a price from 1¢ to 99¢.
  • The price is roughly the crowd's estimate of the chance. Yes at 30¢ means about a 30% chance.
  • Each share you buy pays $1 if your side wins and $0 if it loses. Buy Yes at 30¢ and win: you get $1, a 70¢ profit per share. Lose: you lose the 30¢.
  • You can sell before the result at whatever someone will pay right then.
  • You're betting against other people, often professionals and bots. There's no house that guarantees a fair price.
  • Most markets charge a small fee on each trade. It's biggest when the price is near 50¢.
  • Read the rules on each market. The title is a summary; the rules decide how it's settled.

How this practice account works. When you place a bet, it looks at the live order book (the real list of people offering to sell) and fills your bet at the prices you'd actually get, plus the real fee. Bigger bets get worse prices, just like on the real site. Tap "Update prices" to see what your bets are worth now and to settle any that have finished.

What it can't copy perfectly. Real orders can fail or move the price as other people trade at the same moment; here they fill instantly at the book's prices. Fee rebate programs aren't included.
