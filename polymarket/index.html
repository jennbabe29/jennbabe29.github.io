Polymarket Wallet Check

Do the top Polymarket traders actually have an edge, or did they get lucky? This page pulls their real betting history from Polymarket's public data and checks. It only reads public data. It never asks for or touches a wallet. Practice account →

How to read the results

The core idea. On Polymarket, the price is the crowd's guess at the odds. Buying "Yes" at 60¢ means the market thinks it happens about 60% of the time. If a trader has no edge, buying at fair prices, their profit over many bets should hover around zero, with ups and downs from luck. The question is whether their profit is bigger than luck can reasonably explain, given how much they bet each time.

  • Clean bets: bets held until the market was decided, where the numbers add up (profit = shares × (payout − price paid)). Only these are tested. Bets sold early, or built through the complex trades market makers use, are left out because Polymarket's "price paid" for them isn't reliable. The card shows how much profit came from each group.
  • Win rate vs expected: how often their clean bets won, next to how often the prices they paid said they should win.
  • Edge score: their profit on clean bets compared with how much it would swing from pure luck at those bet sizes. Between −2 and 2: fits luck. 2 to 3: some evidence of an edge. 3 or more: strong evidence it isn't luck. Big bets swing a lot, so a large profit made from a few large bets can still score low.
  • Return on money bet: profit on clean bets divided by the money put into them.
  • "X% of winnings from 3 bets": shown when most of their winnings came from just their 3 biggest wins. That's how lucky streaks usually look.

Limits, honestly. A leaderboard is a list of winners, so some people on it are there by luck alone. With 50 traders, one or two will score above 2 just by chance, which is why only 3+ counts as strong. A trader whose profit comes mostly from trading in and out, or from market making, can have a real edge that this test can't see. The history used is each wallet's most recent finished bets (up to about 3,000), not only the leaderboard period, so it may not include the bets behind their leaderboard profit. Past results never guarantee future ones.

Data: Polymarket public Data API (v2). Figures are in USD (USDC). This is an analysis tool, not financial advice. Prediction-market trading is restricted in some places; check the rules where you live.
