What the bot does

Every 2 hours it wakes up and:

  • Copies skilled traders. It follows the traders that passed the luck-vs-skill test and copies their new bets, unless the price has already moved.
  • Looks for price gaps. Sometimes the prices across all answers to a question add up wrong, so buying every answer locks in a small profit.
  • Reads the news. It checks the news on a few popular questions and bets when it thinks the price is clearly wrong.
  • Sizes every bet with Kelly. Bigger edge, bigger bet. It uses a quarter of the full Kelly amount and never more than 5% of the account on one bet, to avoid blowing up.

Prices, fees and results are real. The money is not. After 2 weeks, check whether it made or lost money before even thinking about real money.
